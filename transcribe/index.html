How to send a file here

BubbyScribe never uploads anything. The recording stays on your device — Whisper runs in your browser.

📱 iPhone / iPad

  1. In Voice Memos (or any app) tap Share → Save to Files.
  2. Open BubbyScribe, tap Choose file → Browse and pick it.
  3. Tip: add BubbyScribe to your Home Screen (Share → Add to Home Screen) so it feels like a real app.

🤖 Android

  1. Install BubbyScribe from Chrome's menu (Add to Home screen).
  2. From any app, tap Share → BubbyScribe. It starts transcribing right away.

💻 Mac / PC

  1. Drag the recording anywhere onto this page, or click Choose file.
  2. Turn on Use graphics chip in Settings for big files — it's a lot faster.

Works with .m4a, .mp3, .wav, .mp4, .mov, .webm and most other audio or video. Very long recordings (2 h+) may be too much for a phone — use a computer for those.

Need to dictate live instead? BubbyFlow does that.
